YouTube Music Nights is a content series that collaborates with artists to film live performances at iconic venues worldwide, including locations in Los Angeles, New York, Paris, London, Tokyo, and artist-selected destinations. The performances are shared exclusively on each artist's Official Artist Channel.

The first wave of Music Nights content features Isaiah Rashad performing at Intuit Plaza in Inglewood, California to celebrate his album release; Kacey Musgraves performing three sold-out shows at Gruene Hall in New Braunfels, Texas; and Bleachers performing at Stone Pony in Asbury Park, New Jersey. Each performance is available in full on the respective artist's official YouTube channel.

Exclusive behind-the-scenes moments from the performances are available on Shorts, directly on each artist's Official Artist Channel. YouTube states more exclusive performances are planned in cities around the world, with creators encouraged to monitor Official Artist Channels for upcoming live moments.
