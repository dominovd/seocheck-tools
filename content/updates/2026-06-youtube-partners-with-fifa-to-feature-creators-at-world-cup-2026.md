FIFA's official media partners will have the option to live stream the first 10 minutes of every World Cup 2026 match on their YouTube channels, and will be able to stream a select number of matches in full. This marks the first time in FIFA World Cup history that such streaming access has been offered.

YouTube will host the YouTube FIFA Creator Cup on July 12, 2026, in New York City, featuring global YouTube creators, elite athletes, and celebrities competing in an exhibition soccer match. The event will stream live globally only on YouTube.

A curated roster of 25 creators from countries including the United States, Mexico, Brazil, United Kingdom, South Korea, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Japan, Belgium, Canada, and the United Arab Emirates will attend matches throughout the tournament. These creators will produce content covering match-day events, local food culture, behind-the-scenes moments, and stadium experiences for their combined 350+ million subscribers.
