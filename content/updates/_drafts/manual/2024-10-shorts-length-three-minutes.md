YouTube extended the Shorts upload length from 60 seconds to three minutes starting October 15, 2024. The change applies to videos uploaded directly through the Shorts camera and to videos uploaded through the standard flow that fall under the new cap.

Shorts above three minutes remain ineligible for the Shorts feed and are treated as standard long-form videos. Music library tracks longer than the previous 60-second limit are now usable inside Shorts without truncation, which opens up format ideas that depended on a full musical phrase.

The Shorts feed retention model continues to favor short attention spans. The longer cap supports formats that needed more setup time, not formats that pad a thin idea into three minutes.
