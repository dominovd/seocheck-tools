YouTube introduced a new Creator Studio upload tool on March 18, 2024 that requires creators to disclose realistic altered or synthetic content. Disclosed videos show a label in the expanded description, and a more prominent label appears on sensitive topics like health, news, elections, and finance.

The requirement covers content that depicts a real person saying or doing something they did not do, alters footage of an actual place or event, or generates a realistic-looking scene that did not occur. Clearly artistic effects, animation, color correction, beauty filters, and routine production tools do not require disclosure.

The policy targets misleading content; it does not remove monetization from disclosed videos. Failing to disclose required content can result in penalties including removal and Partner Program suspension. YouTube is also working on automatic detection for content that should have been disclosed.
