YouTube made automatic dubbing generally available in Creator Studio during late 2024. The initial rollout covered nine languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Indonesian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Hindi.

The dub is generated automatically from the original audio track using YouTube's AI translation pipeline. Creators preview the dubbed tracks in Creator Studio before they go live and can disable specific languages or replace them with a manually produced track. Viewers hear the dub that matches their YouTube language setting by default and can switch tracks from the player.

Auto-dub eligibility is currently limited to knowledge-based channels with a track record of policy-compliant uploads. Music, gaming with heavy real-time commentary, and channels with frequent strikes are not in the initial eligible cohort. The AI voice approximates the original speaker's tone where supported but is not a full voice clone.
