All official warm-up matches for the ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026 have already streamed live globally on the ICC's YouTube channel. Every match of the tournament will be available live in multiple territories: Nepal, Germany, the Netherlands, and Japan. Dedicated audience development campaigns will also run across Malaysia, France, Italy, Hong Kong, South Korea, Indonesia, and Thailand, with additional support for fan engagement in Belgium, Switzerland, Denmark, Finland, Portugal, Spain, and Sweden.

The YouTube offering includes local language content and commentary options. Dedicated feeds will be available in Japanese, Bahasa Indonesia, and Thai, building on similar initiatives tested during the ICC Men's T20 World Cup 2026.

Full match highlights and video-on-demand content will be available globally throughout the event. The partnership aims to make cricket more accessible and discoverable for audiences worldwide, particularly in emerging markets where interest in the sport is growing.
